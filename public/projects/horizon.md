This is a work in progress game engine I initially started to learn vulkan. Having completed multiple openGL projects in the past, I wanted to understand a different rendering API, and decided to primarily target vulkan due to its cross platform nature. I also set out with the goal of allowing a user to choose between the two APIs similar to modern commercial engines. 

THis page will be updated as more progress is made on the engine.
